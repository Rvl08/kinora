They are **not** part of the repository and are not distributed with Kinora; they are used for
testing only. This file gives the credits their licences ask for. Sources and hashes are pinned in
[`manifest.json`](manifest.json).

## Generated by Kinora

`video-h264-1080p30-vfr`, `video-hevc-2160p60-hdr10`, `video-prores422-2160p`, `video-av1-1080p30`,
`video-vp9-alpha-1080p30` and `video-screen-1440p30-lossless` are synthesised by `generate.mjs` from
FFmpeg test sources (`testsrc2`, `sine`, `color`) and text drawn with the system's Consolas font. They
are covered by the repository licence (MIT OR Apache-2.0). The screen recording shows the text of
the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE-APACHE`).
